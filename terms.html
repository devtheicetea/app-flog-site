What Flog is

Flog is a logging app for film photographers. It helps you record each frame as you shoot it — camera, lens, film stock, location, light, and exposure metadata, with an in-app reference snap — then import your developed scans later and reconcile them with the frames you logged.

Flog is a personal record-keeping tool. It does not develop, scan, or store your film, and it makes no guarantee about the accuracy of any metadata you enter or that the app derives.

What's free, what's Pro

Flog is free to download and use. You can log rolls and frames, capture reference snaps, build your camera and lens gear, import and reconcile scans, browse your archive, and export your frames.

The free tier lets you keep one active roll in progress at a time. Flog Pro removes that limit, so you can load and shoot unlimited rolls in flight. The exact set of Pro features may change over time.
