Flog

Film Logbook for iPhone

A logbook for every frame of film you shoot.

Flog records the story behind each frame — camera, film stock, place, light, and exposure — then helps you pair your developed scans back to the shots you took.

Download on the App Store iPhone · iOS 18.0+

What it does

Shooting film means waiting weeks to see your photos, by which time the details are gone. Flog captures them in the moment: as you shoot, log the camera, the film stock, where you are, the light, and your exposure settings, with a quick in-app reference snap so you remember the shot.

When your scans come back, import them and Flog helps you pair each one to the frame you logged — so every photo carries the story of how it was made.

Features

  • Frame logCamera, film stock, location, light, and exposure (aperture, shutter, ISO) for every frame — with an in-app reference snap, Live Photo, and the weather at the moment you shot.
  • RollsTrack each roll through its life: loaded, finished, at the lab, developed, archived. Disposable and instant cameras supported too.
  • ScansImport your developed scans and pair them back to the right frame, with Vision-powered matching.
  • Formats35mm, half-frame, 120 (6×4.5, 6×6, 6×7, 6×9), 110, large format (4×5, 8×10), and instant — Polaroid and Instax.
  • GearA curated catalog of 60+ cameras. Build your shelf and load rolls straight from your own gear.
  • ArchiveBrowse your shots by timeline, map, film stock, camera, or year — and search across everything.
  • ShareExport single frames in editorial styles, plus double exposures and collages.
  • LanguagesEnglish, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Dutch, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Swedish, Turkish, Arabic, and both Chinese variants.

Who it's for

If you shoot 35mm, 120, or instant film and want to remember more than just the photo — which lens, which stock, where you stood, what the light was doing — Flog is the notebook for it. Keep a clean record of every roll, and bring your scans home to the frames they belong to.
